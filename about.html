Our Story of Resilience & Trust

From humble beginnings in Port Harcourt to becoming a trusted name in responsible lending across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Journey Since 2010

Sixteen years of innovation, setbacks, learning, and ultimately — excellence in serving our community.

2010

Founded in Port Harcourt

Simple Cooperation began as a small cooperative lending initiative helping local traders and families access fair credit during a time when traditional banks were often out of reach for everyday Nigerians.

2011–2015

Challenges & Backslides

Economic volatility, regulatory uncertainty in the microfinance sector, high default rates during fuel subsidy removal periods, and intense competition from informal lenders tested our model. We faced periods of slowed growth, capital constraints, and the painful reality of writing off bad loans while maintaining trust with honest borrowers.

2016–2020

Turning Point & Digital Pivot

We embraced technology, strengthened risk assessment, partnered with reliable telcos and payment gateways, and focused on transparency. By 2018 we had rebuilt trust, expanded to multiple states, and began offering faster, fairer loans — including crypto and wire options for global Nigerians.

2021–2026

Excellence & Impact

Today we proudly stand with:

  • Over ₦45 billion disbursed in responsible loans
  • More than 85,000 families and businesses supported
  • 99.2% repayment rate built on trust, not coercion
  • Expanded to crypto, wire transfers, and cross-border support
  • Recognized for transparency and customer-first approach in Nigeria's evolving fintech landscape

Our Enduring Mission

We exist to make responsible credit accessible, transparent, and empowering — never exploitative. Every loan is backed by human oversight, fair terms, and genuine care for our clients' long-term financial health.
