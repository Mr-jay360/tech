Michael Thompson’s Journey

After graduating from the University of Lagos in 2008, I watched too many families lose everything during the financial crisis. That experience drove me to create a company that would never put profit above people.

Today, Simple Cooperation has disbursed over ₦42 billion in responsible loans and maintains a 99.8% repayment rate — not because we chase borrowers, but because we choose them carefully and support them every step of the way.
